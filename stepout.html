Overview

How can we help youth reduce digital overstimulation and regain focus?

Problem Statement

Students 16–23 spend the vast majority of their time online and in isolation, causing a lack of focus, poor sleep, and disconnection. They need to balance their digital and outdoor life to overcome overstimulation, lack of motivation, and screen exhaustion.

This project aims to create a free mobile app that helps youth balance their outdoor and digital life. It will be designed for wellness, with progress tracking that motivates users to stay consistent.

Demographic Insight

Target

Students in high school or post-secondary (16–23) who find themselves in a cycle of bad habits. They want to take control of their lifestyle, but have a hard time staying consistent, and want guidance toward maintaining a good balance.

Research Insight

Balancing Screen Time with Outdoor Habit Building

Excessive screen time growth correlates directly with reduced concentration and psychological distress. Existing wellness apps rely heavily on subscriptions or complex vision-boarding, leaving a gap for a solution that focuses on daily outdoor micro-habits without added financial friction.

Sleep & Recovery

Heavy late-night screen time disrupts circadian rhythms; StepOut encourages daily outdoor completion early in the day to improve sleep quality and reduce fatigue.

Focus & Balance

Structured, active daily challenges improve focus and satisfaction; other apps derive users away from real habit formation.

Zero-Barrier Access

Built as a non-profit app to eliminate subscription barriers, so youth can build outdoor habits without financial friction.

Solution

StepOut: the mobile app helping youth balance digital life and outdoor activity.

Streamlines daily habit building through structured outdoor micro-tasks, progress tracking, and zero-barrier access.

Rebalance digital habits by encouraging daily outdoor micro-actions.

StepOut is a free, non-profit mobile app that generates personalized outdoor tasks, tracks progress and consistency, and helps students record their life balance.

Design Process & Iteration

Evolving StepOut across wireframes, testing, and high-fidelity UI

User Testing & Feedback

Refining user onboarding and core daily challenge interactions

I ran usability sessions and gathered feedback to refine the onboarding flow and daily challenge interactions. Testing surfaced opportunities around navigation clarity, layout hierarchy, and accessibility.

Navigation & Layout

Reorganized navigation to shorten the distance between onboarding and the first outdoor task.

Survey & Onboarding

Simplified onboarding survey wording to reduce drop-off before the first outdoor challenge.

Accessibility & Contrast

Adjusted color contrast and touch target sizing to improve readability and accessibility.

Reflection & Future Roadmap

Building a Sustainable, Ethical Product for Long-Term Impact

StepOut sits at a similar intersection to gamified digital wellness apps, but is guided by a non-profit, privacy-first ethos: no ads, no subscriptions, and no monetized data-selling business model.

Ultimately, my priority in designing StepOut was to give youth a genuinely accessible, non-punitive way to rebuild outdoor habits — something that respects their time, their data, and their pace, rather than optimizing for screen time itself.
